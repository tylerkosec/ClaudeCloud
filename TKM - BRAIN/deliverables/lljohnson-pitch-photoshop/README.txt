LL JOHNSON PITCH: PHOTOSHOP TEMPLATE
Tyler Kosec Media, October 2026

WHAT THIS IS
LLJ_Pitch_Template.jsx is a Photoshop script. It builds the 10-slide pitch deck
(1920x1080) as layered, fully editable PSDs and drops your stills into the photo
frames. Nothing is flattened: every word is a text layer, every color block is a
fill layer you can recolor, and every photo is a smart object.

BEFORE YOU RUN IT (2 minutes)
1. Install the two free fonts, or Photoshop will use Arial instead:
   - Oswald: fonts.google.com/specimen/Oswald (install the "SemiBold" weight from the static folder)
   - Public Sans: fonts.google.com/specimen/Public+Sans (Regular, SemiBold, Bold)
   Restart Photoshop after installing.
2. Put the stills in order. Photos go into the frames in file-name order (A to Z),
   so rename the six you want as 01_, 02_ ... 06_:
   01  Cover, full screen. Keep the subject on the RIGHT, because the title sits on the left.
       A machine working is ideal.
   02  Slide 2, right side, tall crop (740 x 1080). A close detail or a person works best.
   03  Slide 7, big photo (1080 x 510). Your best work sample.
   04  Slide 7, small top right (560 x 243).
   05  Slide 7, small bottom right (560 x 243).
   06  Slide 10, full-screen background. It gets darkened, so any wide shot works.
   Extra stills in the folder are ignored.

RUN IT
Photoshop > File > Scripts > Browse... > LLJ_Pitch_Template.jsx
  Dialog 1: pick F:\TORO\STILLS
  Dialog 2: pick where to save (an empty folder is best)
It saves 01-cover.psd ... 10-next-step.psd, a JPG of each, and
"LL Johnson Pitch - Tyler Kosec Media.pdf" with all 10 pages.
It takes a minute or two. A message tells you how many stills it placed and
whether any fonts were missing.

EDITING
- Text: double-click any text layer. Layers are named by what they are ("Headline 1", "Row 2 label").
- Colors: double-click a fill layer's thumbnail.
- Swap a photo: right-click the PHOTO layer > Replace Contents. It stays clipped to its frame.
  Move or scale it with Ctrl+T.
- Darker or lighter photo overlay: double-click "Scrim (gradient...)" on slides 1 and 10.
- After editing, re-make the PDF with File > Automate > PDF Presentation.

FILL THESE IN BEFORE YOU SEND
- Slide 10: your phone number (the layer "Contact (add your phone)").
- Slide 3: check each number on your phone first (TikTok likes, LinkedIn followers,
  Facebook pages). The counts came from search results, not the live pages.
  There's a hidden layer "OPTIONAL Row 3" for your TikTok follower count: type it in, then turn the layer on.
- Slide 2: optional hidden caption for the photo. Edit it, then turn it on.
- Slide 7: the caption says "Stills from my Toro shoots." Change it if that's not what the photos are.
- Slide 8: confirm "2 shoot days a month" and "about an hour a week" match your offer.

NOTE
The "See my work" button on slide 7 is a picture of a button. Photoshop PDFs don't carry links,
so paste the reel link into your email instead.
