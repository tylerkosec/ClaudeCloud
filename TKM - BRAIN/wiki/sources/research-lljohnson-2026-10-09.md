## 3. Engagement rate

**Not calculated.** It needs the numbers on each post, and no platform's post pages could be loaded. Nothing here is estimated.

**Method to use when the data is pulled** (per brief):
- Per post: (likes + comments + shares + saves where public) ÷ followers at time of pull.
- **Median**, not average, across posts from the last 30–60 days.
- Exclude posts under 7 days old (cutoff: anything after 2026-10-02 if pulled today).
- Pinned posts listed separately, not in the median.
- Report sample size (n) and pull date next to every figure.

**Benchmarks to compare against (source named; verify against the original before quoting to John):**
- **Rival IQ, 2025 Social Media Industry Benchmark Report.** Median engagement per post by followers, all industries: Instagram ~0.36%, TikTok ~1.73%, Facebook under 0.1%. I only saw these in secondary summaries, not the PDF itself. PDF: get.rivaliq.com/hubfs/eBooks/2025-Social-Media-Industry-Benchmark-Report.pdf. Method: 150 random companies per industry, interactions ÷ followers.
- **Socialinsider**: Instagram 0.48% *average* for 2025 (likes + comments ÷ followers, 35M posts). This is an average, not a median, so it is less comparable.
- **Buffer (Sept 2025)**: Instagram tier medians of ~5.2% for accounts under 1K, falling with size. Its denominator includes saves and shares, so don't mix it with Rival IQ.
- Caveat: these sources differ by up to 10× because of definitions. Pick one (Rival IQ, median) and match its formula exactly.

## The pitch, short

### 3 numbers that show the gap (the ones that hold up today)
1. **86 LinkedIn followers, on a page still named "Pattlen Enterprises".** LinkedIn is where superintendents, parks managers and contractors' owners are, and the page still uses the old holding company's name 17 months after the May 2025 sale. *Fair if checked:* it's their own page; he can open it.
2. **One brand, 3+ Facebook pages:** main page ~1.2K followers, a Colorado Springs page with **6**, and at least two more indexed LL Johnson pages. *Fair if checked:* searching "L.L. Johnson Distributing" on Facebook shows them. Split pages divide followers and confuse customers searching for them.
3. **50 years, 22 Toro awards, 4 branches → 2,851 Instagram followers.** *Fair if checked:* the awards come from Colorado Green (2018), the founding year from the 2025 acquisition coverage, and the follower count from their own profile. The point isn't that 2,851 is bad. Their story is far bigger than their audience.

The sharpest number, **median engagement per post vs. Rival IQ benchmark**, is not verified yet. Pull it before the meeting.

### 3 content ideas built on hidden assets
Their winners are unverified, so these build on the topics they already chose to post (shop work, new Toro tech) plus the assets above.
1. **"50 Years, 50 Faces"** (2026 anniversary series). 20–40s vertical clips: one employee or longtime customer each, "how long, what changed, best day on the job". Start with John (25 yrs). It builds trust in the people, and the anniversary has a natural end date.
2. **"In the Shop"** (Service Solutions Center). Weekly 30s reels: bedknife grinding, reel setup, Dingo/Ventrac pre-season checks, a tech explaining one fix. It extends the shop video they already made, and it shows a service capability that box-store competitors can't claim.
3. **"Robot on the Course"** (Toro autonomous mowers in Colorado). Film one local course running a Turf Pro: time-lapse, a superintendent on camera, the LL Johnson rep who set it up. It extends the autonomous-mower post they already made, and it's new tech golf buyers are actively curious about (Turf Pro 200 launched June 2026).

*Seasonal add-on (now):* golf irrigation winterization/blowout. It ties straight to the 2018 Best in Irrigation award.

### The money line
When more superintendents, parks managers and contractors see the people, the shop and 50 years of Toro work, more of them already trust LL Johnson before they pick up the phone. Reach and trust shorten the path to a call. No lead count promised.
